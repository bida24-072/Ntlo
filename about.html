Ntlo is Botswana's map-first property platform — built in Gaborone, for Botswana.

Built by Batswana,
for Botswana.

Ntlo began with a simple frustration: property hunting in Botswana was stuck in the past. Endless PDF listings. Long chains of WhatsApp messages. No maps, no filters, no transparency.

So we built something better. A platform where you explore homes by neighbourhood, filter by what matters to you, and see exactly where you'd be living — before you ever pick up the phone.

Today, Ntlo lists hundreds of properties across Gaborone, Francistown, Maun, and Kasane. Every listing is verified. Every agent is local. Every map pin is real.

Why the map?

Because location is the most important decision in buying a home. Not just the suburb — the street, the neighbours, the walk to the shops, the school run.

Our map shows you everything at once. Aerial views. Satellite imagery. Distance to schools, hospitals, malls, and main roads. You can see the real Botswana, not just the listing photos.

Whether you're buying your first apartment in Extension 12 or looking for a family home in Phakalane, Ntlo gives you the full picture.
